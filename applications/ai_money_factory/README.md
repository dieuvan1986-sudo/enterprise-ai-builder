# AI Money Factory

> AI-powered business operating system built on Enterprise AI Builder.

---

# Vision

AI Money Factory is a practical AI system designed to help a solo entrepreneur research products, create content, automate repetitive work and increase online revenue.

Enterprise AI Builder provides the runtime and infrastructure.

AI Money Factory provides real business capabilities.

---

# Objectives

- Research products automatically
- Build a product knowledge base
- Generate TikTok content
- Generate Facebook content
- Generate YouTube Shorts ideas
- Manage prompt templates
- Operate through Telegram
- Support future workflow automation

# Core Modules

## Product Intelligence

Maintain structured knowledge for every product.

Examples:

- Product profile
- Customer pain points
- Competitors
- Benefits
- Clinical evidence
- Selling points
- FAQ

---

## Research Agent

Responsible for:

- Collecting information
- Organizing information
- Updating knowledge
- Preparing structured reports

---

## Content Agent

Responsible for:

- Hook generation
- Script writing
- Caption generation
- CTA generation
- Content calendar

---

## Telegram Assistant

Provides a simple interface for interacting with the system.

Examples:

- Research a product
- Generate a TikTok script
- Show daily tasks
- Display business reports

---

# Knowledge First
